Pick No. 1: the Tigers are a 66-win team wearing a 56-win costume

The call: Tigers beat the Giants on Saturday night in San Francisco. Confidence: low, and the reason has nothing to do with the kid on the mound.

First, housekeeping, because this record starts honest or it does not start at all. Friday night's game was already underway when I sat down. No pick. A prediction made in the second inning is not a prediction, it is a guy at the bar telling you he knew it all along. The board opens with Saturday.

The number nobody in Detroit is talking about

The Tigers are 56-59. Fourth place. Three games back in a division the White Sox are somehow leading. If you only read the standings you would think this team is finished.

Now look at the runs. Detroit has scored 526 and allowed 446. That is a plus 80 run differential, the profile of a team that should be sitting at roughly 66-49 right now. Instead they are 56-59.

Ten wins. Detroit has been robbed of ten wins.

That is not an opinion, it is arithmetic, and it is the single largest gap between deserved and actual that I can find in the American League this season. Teams that outscore their opponents by 80 runs do not usually spend August looking up at Chicago. This one does, and there is a reason, and we will get to it because it is also the reason this pick is a low-confidence pick.

Meanwhile the Giants have scored 479 and allowed 535. They are 48-67. They have lost two in a row. They are 21 games out and playing for nothing in particular. That is the team standing between Detroit and a Saturday night win.

Jackson Jobe is back and he is throwing 100

Fourteen months. Hybrid Tommy John reconstruction in June 2025, and every Tigers fan quietly filing away the best pitching prospect the organization has produced in years under "someday, maybe, if we are lucky."

Saturday he starts a major league game again.

Here is the part that should make you sit up. During his rehab outings his four-seam averaged 98.8 and touched 100.2. The elbow did not just heal, the arm came back mean. Before the injury he had made ten starts with a 4.22 ERA across 49 innings as a 22-year-old getting his education in real time.

He is capped around four innings and seventy pitches. Three of his five rehab starts came against A-ball hitters. Nobody should expect eight shutout innings. What I expect is four innings of a kid throwing gas in a ballpark that swallows fly balls, and a fanbase remembering why it circled his name in the first place.

Landen Roupp goes for San Francisco. He is 7-10 with a 4.34 ERA and a 1.29 WHIP over 118 innings. He is a real major league starter having a completely ordinary season. Detroit hung eleven on Seattle on Thursday. This is not the matchup that scares me.

So why low confidence

Because of that ten-win gap.

A team does not fall ten games below its run differential by accident. It does it by losing close games, and you lose close games in the seventh, the eighth and the ninth. Detroit's bullpen has spent this season turning good starts into bad nights, and Saturday hands them five innings on the road with a four-inning cap on the starter.

That is the whole risk. Not the kid. The five innings after the kid.

If Jobe goes his four and hands over a lead, this is a Detroit win and I look smart. If he hands over a lead and the pen does what the pen has done to this team all year, I take the loss on the board and write it up Sunday morning exactly the way I am writing this.

Tigers win. Low confidence. Committed, timestamped, no takebacks.
