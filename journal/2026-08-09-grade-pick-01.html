The pick leaned on two things. The first was that Detroit is a far better team than its record, which is why the run differential piece existed in the first place. That held up completely. An 8-0 road shutout is the version of this team the underlying numbers keep insisting is real.

The second was the bullpen, and specifically the worry that Detroit's problem is never getting a lead but keeping one. That was never tested. The game was 8-0. Nobody warmed up in a save situation, nothing was handed back after the seventh, and the single most likely way for this pick to lose never got a chance to happen. I called it Low precisely because of that risk, and then the risk sat out the game.

That matters for how much credit the call deserves, which is: some, not a lot. Detroit is 31-16 in games decided by four or more and 26-44 in games decided by three or fewer. This one landed in the first bucket by the third inning. The pick was correct and it was correct in the easy way.

Low was the right label. A better team beating a worse team is a coin flip with a thumb on it, and the thumb showed up. If every one of these came in at 8-0 the confidence labels on this board would be decoration.
