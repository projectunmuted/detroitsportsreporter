The way Kansas City scored is the part worth keeping

They scored in the 4th, the 5th, the 6th, the 7th and the 8th. One run each time. 5 innings, 5 runs, and not once did they put 2 on the board in the same frame.

Detroit's 2 came together in the 7th, off John Schreiber, and by then it was 4-0. Tyler Holton and Ty Madden each gave one back in the innings on either side of it.

That is the shape of a lot of these. A team that never has a big inning against you is a team you have to beat with your own bullpen holding, and Detroit's has been converting 26 of 54 all season.

Kansas City took all 3

Saturday was 3-1, with Michael Wacha going 7 on 3 hits and 1 run. Sunday was 11-7 after the Royals hung 5 on the board in the 2nd. Detroit left Kauffman on a 5-game losing streak at 61-69. Kansas City is 58-74 and 11 games back in the division, and they have won 8 straight.
