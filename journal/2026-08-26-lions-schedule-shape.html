August 26, 2026 · Detroit Lions · Analysis

We're the only team in the league with a short week and a bye inside the first 6 weeks

I went looking at the 2026 schedule for something other than who we play, because everybody's already done who we play. What's underneath it is the shape, and the shape here is a little strange.

Detroit opens at home against New Orleans on Sunday September 13 at 1:00pm. Then 4 days later we're in Buffalo on Thursday night. That's the earliest short week anybody in the NFL plays this year, and the only other team playing it is the Bills, because they're the ones we're playing. Nobody else has a game on 4 days rest until week 3.

Then the bye shows up in week 6. Only Carolina and Kansas City get one earlier.

I pulled all 32 schedules to see if anybody else drew that combination, and nobody did.

6 teams get an early bye. 10 teams get a short week in the first 6 weeks. We're in both lists and we're the only ones.

The part I actually care about

The short week is 1 game. The bye is the whole back half of the year.

An early bye is just arithmetic. Whatever week it lands in, you play 18 minus that many games afterward with no break at all. Week 6 means 12 straight, which ties us with Cincinnati, Miami and Minnesota for 3rd most. Carolina and Kansas City are at 13. At the other end Arizona and Dallas don't take theirs until week 14 and finish with 4.

So from October 25 against Green Bay through the finale at Lambeau on January 10, there's no week off. The only thing in there that looks like a breather is the Thanksgiving game against Chicago, and that's a short week, not a long one. It cuts the other way.

Nobody in the league is off in week 12, which is Thanksgiving week, and that's the one part of this that's pretty clearly on purpose.

What I'd hedge on

I don't want to oversell it. Teams win with early byes and teams fall apart with late ones, and I haven't gone and checked whether the effect is real, because I'd want a lot more than 1 season of it before I said anything either way. This is a description of the schedule, not a prediction about it.

What I'd say is that it front loads when the roster has to be right. If we're still figuring things out in November there's no scheduled week to figure them out in, and the injury math over a 12 game run with no break probably isn't the same as over 7. That's the version of this I actually believe.

Kickoff times can still move late in the year, since the league flexes games in December. Byes don't move, and a flex can't turn a Sunday game into a Thursday one, so the 2 numbers this whole thing rests on are fixed.

Preseason finishes Saturday at Indianapolis, 1:00pm. Then it's 2 weeks and this starts.
