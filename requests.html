Answered

Questions readers have already asked, and where the answer went.

  • Detroit's preseason record against their regular season, just this franchise, as a scatter plot. Is there a trend line at all?

    25 seasons, correlation +0.285, and a permutation test says a correlation that strong turns up by chance about 1 time in 6. Drop 2008 and it jumps to +0.514, which is the actual finding. Read the answer

    Asked 2026-08-13 in an r/detroitlions thread

  • Out of the teams that won all their preseason games, how would they be grouped by total wins? What does that histogram look like?

    68 teams out of 798, and there's no cluster anywhere. Win totals run 0 to 14, and 45.6% of them won 9 or more per 17 against 46.9% of everybody else. Read the answer

    Asked 2026-08-13 in an r/detroitlions thread

  • The 2008 Lions went 4-0 in the preseason and 0-16. The sample starts in 2015, so it's missing.

    They were right and the data goes back to 2000, not 2015. Rerun on 798 team-seasons instead of 320. The headline held, the best line in the original didn't, and 2 data defects turned up underneath. Read the answer

    Asked 2026-08-13 in an r/detroitlions thread

  • Cleveland and Chicago are the worst teams in baseball at throwing out runners. Time to let Max show off those wheels.

    Both halves of the premise checked out and the answer still came back the other way. Cleveland's 15.7% is 2 catchers averaged into 1 number, and the good one had been catching. Read the answer
